# BluffTheSpot (BST) Preflop Strategy Reference

## Overview
BluffTheSpot (BST) is a poker training platform offering strategy from elite No Limit Hold'em pros. Their "Preflop Bible" series is a comprehensive guide to preflop play.

## Core Philosophy: Raise or Fold (No Limping)

### BST advocates for a "raise or fold" approach:
- **Never limp-enter a pot** - Always enter with a raise
- **Simplifies the game tree** - Reduces complex decision points
- **Puts pressure on opponents** - Forces them to make difficult decisions
- **Additional ways to win** - Can win blinds preflop without seeing a flop

> "When everyone folds and you decide to enter the hand, always do so with a raise and never limp."

## Key Strategy Elements

### 1. Small Blind: 3-Bet or Fold
- From the Small Blind, BST recommends playing a "3bet-or-fold" strategy
- This increases pot size with a strong range
- Avoids playing weak hands out of position

### 2. Big Blind Defense
- **Don't overfold!** - Many micro/low stakes players fold too much preflop
- Defend with appropriate frequency against opens
- Consider position and opener's range

### 3. Linear 4-Bet Strategy
- Prefers a linear 4-bet strategy that blurs value/bluffs
- Hands with mixed strategies: AQs-ATs, KQs-KTs, JJ, TT, AQo
- Polarized approach in certain spots

### 4. Position-Based Play
- Strong focus on positional awareness
- Different ranges by position (EP, MP, LP, Blinds)
- Late position can play wider range

## Key Differences from GTO

| Aspect | BST Approach | Traditional GTO |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Limping | Never limp | Some limp spots exist |
| Strategy | Raise or Fold | May include mixed strategies |
| Complexity | Simplified game tree | Complete game tree |
| Focus | Practical exploitation | Theoretical balance |
